# Research notes · 5 October 2026

## Angel One integration update · 14 October 2026

The owner's existing Trading_Bot project uses Angel One credentials. This app now supports Angel One market data in paper mode. Endpoint paths and authentication/quote/Greek payloads were verified against the [official SmartAPI Python client](https://github.com/angel-one/smartapi-python/blob/main/SmartApi/smartConnect.py) and the [official option Greeks announcement](https://smartapi.angelbroking.com/smartapi/forum/topic/4254/announcing-option-greeks-api-for-smartapi-users). Contract metadata comes from Angel's public instrument master. The adapter uses FULL quotes for exchange timestamps, order-book bid/ask, volume and open interest, with Greeks fetched separately and matched to the requested expiry. No Angel order endpoint is implemented. Live session verification results must distinguish after-hours API access from fresh tradable quotes during an open session.

The after-hours connection check on 14 October succeeded: authentication, 1,826 NIFTY option contracts, both index quotes, four sampled option quotes with depth, and 188 option Greek records. These are API-access checks, not evidence of intraday fill correctness or strategy performance. The dashboard was started in Angel paper mode with live execution disabled; the first fresh market-session snapshot and complete simulated entry/exit remain pending.

The sources below were checked while defining this first version. Development agents were assigned broker/rules, open-source systems and strategy research in parallel. The broker and open-source agents completed independent reviews; the strategy agent hit a model-capacity error, so strategy source review was completed directly. Social posts were treated as leads for failure modes, not performance evidence.

## Market and regulation

- [SEBI's August 2026 research release](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2026/1787236407005.pdf) reports that 87.7% of individual equity-derivatives traders lost money in FY26; aggregate net losses were about ₹91,685 crore, and transaction costs were about ₹25,000 crore. About 92% of aggregate individual losses came from options. This argues for strict capital caps, low trade frequency, realistic costs and a paper-first rollout. It does **not** identify a profitable strategy.
- [NSE's retail algo implementation standards](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INVG67858.pdf) require broker-controlled API access, a registered static IP for client generated algos, daily logout of API sessions, identification of API algo orders, an audit trail, and a threshold of at most 10 orders per second for algos that do not need individual registration. This engine's order frequency is far below that threshold; broker-side registration/tagging remains the broker's responsibility.
- [NSE's retail algo FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/FAQ_Retail%20Algo_03112025_NSE.pdf) clarifies the client static IP arrangement and says algo **market orders are not permitted**. Its IOC prohibition is stated for the commodity segment; this engine trades NSE equity index options using protected limit IOC requests. Broker acceptance must still be checked in its sandbox.
- [NSE contract specifications](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-contract-specifications) are subject to changes in expiry days, lot sizes and permitted contracts. The bot reads expiry and lot size from Upstox instead of hardcoding them.

## Broker integration

- [Upstox option contracts](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-option-contracts/) provide current instrument keys, expiries, strikes and lot sizes. The [put/call option chain](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-pc-option-chain/) provides bid, ask, volume, open interest and Greeks. [Full quote V3](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-full-market-quote-v3/) supplies NIFTY and India VIX quotes and a quote timestamp.
- [Place Order V3](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/place-order/) supports protected limit orders and returns broker order IDs. [Order details](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-order-details/) provides status, filled quantity and average price. The engine uses a unique tag, IOC limit request, fill check and position reconciliation. Network or broker uncertainty halts the engine rather than submitting another entry.
- The option-chain endpoint is expiry-specific. If an option remains open after an unfilled exit, the feed requests its held expiry even after that expiry becomes ineligible for new entries. The broker agent found and verified this risk during review.
- [Upstox's April 2026 algo announcement](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/announcements/algo-trading-circular/) says API order traffic must originate from a registered static IP. [Upstox token documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-token/) describes the 3:30 AM next-day expiry **and an official TOTP plus PIN endpoint** for issuing a new token. The bot can renew automatically through that endpoint if the owner opts to supply the app key, PIN and TOTP seed. Those secrets must be protected; a broker rejection or uncertain order still requires human review.
- [Upstox's Analytics Token](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/analytics-token/) lasts one year and is read-only. It supports the market quote and option-chain APIs needed by paper mode and cannot place orders, so it is the preferred credential for longer paper observation.
- [Upstox rate limits](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/rate-limiting/) allow 10 order requests per second for regular algos, with additional minute and 30-minute limits. The engine makes at most one IOC order per decision and polls quotes every 15 seconds. Its real broker adapter remains unverified until tested with the user's sandbox and account.
- [Upstox sandbox](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/sandbox/) provides an order lifecycle testing environment with separate credentials. It should be used for order placement, partial fill, reject, cancel and reconciliation checks before exchange access is enabled.

## Open source and community review

- [OpenAlgo](https://github.com/marketcalls/openalgo) is the strongest general-purpose open-source reference found: a self-hosted platform with broker plugins, strategy hosting, analyzer mode and a React dashboard. Its [design notes](https://github.com/marketcalls/openalgo/blob/main/docs/design/README.md) show useful separation between strategy, broker connectivity and sandbox execution. This project adopts those **architectural ideas**, but no OpenAlgo code, dependency or claimed performance. A direct Upstox adapter keeps the first version smaller and easier to audit. OpenAlgo's AGPL license also requires care before copying or embedding its code into another product.
- [Zerodha's official pykiteconnect SDK](https://github.com/zerodha/pykiteconnect) is a maintained alternative broker integration. Its [static IP guidance](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/static-ip) says API order placement requires a static IP from April 2026. It is not wired into this first version because Upstox exposes option-chain bid/ask and Greeks in a single documented endpoint.
- [Upstox's official Python SDK](https://github.com/upstox/upstox-python) is another maintained reference for API shapes. The implementation here uses a small standard-library HTTP adapter so order submission, status polling and failure handling are visible in one file and require no third-party runtime package.
- [A TradingView NIFTY strategy description](https://in.tradingview.com/script/kGi1yAqr-NIFTY-Options-Regime-Aware-Directional-System/) explicitly notes its long historical chart is a **spot proxy**, not actual option P&L. The bot therefore records and replays option bid/ask quotes rather than treating index-point backtests as option returns.
- [Reddit discussions of paper versus live options execution](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndianStreetBets/comments/1wps8mr/built_my_own_niftybankniftysensex_options_bot_and/) and [operational failures](https://www.reddit.com/r/IndianStreetBets/comments/1wqlgw7/whats_the_worst_operational_not_strategy_failure/) repeatedly mention slippage, wide spreads and broker state surprises. These are anecdotes, not audited results; they informed the spread filter, friction scenarios and halt-on-uncertainty behavior. X and Quora did not yield verifiable strategy evidence in the available search results, and Quora blocked access.

## What remains unproven

The 8/21 EMA plus momentum rule is a transparent baseline to exercise the system. No historical option dataset or live account was available, so no out-of-sample performance claim can be made. The synthetic demonstration is a software test only. Before live use, capture real bid/ask history across different volatility and expiry conditions; run the replay with adverse execution assumptions; forward test in paper mode; test broker order lifecycle in the Upstox sandbox; confirm exact fees/taxes and static-IP configuration; and reconcile P&L with actual broker records.


## ORB implementation update — 14 October 2026

The user selected a ₹20,000 paper account and a review-only agent. Implemented `orb-v1`: 15-minute NIFTY opening range, two closed-minute confirmations, buffered range re-entry stop, 2R index target, ₹400 maximum planned per-trade risk and ₹800 daily lock. Parameter choices are research assumptions. They have not been optimized or validated against historical NIFTY option bid/ask data.

This differs from the [QuantConnect Stocks in Play implementation](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/p1): NIFTY index options instead of a US stock universe; 15-minute range instead of 5; no relative-volume stock selection; option delta-based sizing and a rupee-loss exit instead of stock share sizing. Results from that source cannot be transferred to this bot.

Implementation references: [Angel official SDK candle route](https://github.com/angel-one/smartapi-python/blob/main/SmartApi/smartConnect.py), [Upstox intraday candles V3](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-intra-day-candle-data/), and [Gemini generateContent API](https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content). Candle data is filtered to the same session and completed bars only. The reviewer output has no trading authority. The direct Gemini key setting was empty. A subsequent inspection found the other project’s `agy --print` Antigravity integration; the local CLI successfully returned the requested connection-check response. The reviewer now supports that signed-in CLI route using plan mode, sandbox restrictions, an isolated working directory and a credential-filtered environment.

Validation must include costs, skipped unaffordable lots, actual lot/expiry changes, trade count, drawdowns and false-break frequency. A high win rate alone is not evidence of positive expectancy. LLM commentary is not an edge detector and should not be treated as a substitute for out-of-sample or forward tests.

## Strategy research update — 27 October 2026

Question: which documented intraday strategy is worth adding next to ORB for long NIFTY options? Findings:

- **Noise-area intraday momentum** ([Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon, SFI WP 24-97](https://www.sfi.ch/de/publications/n-24-97-beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-s-p500-etf-spy)) gives exact, reproducible rules: a 14-day time-of-day band around the open (gap-adjusted), decisions only at half-hour marks, a band/VWAP trailing stop and exit at the close. For SPY 2007–2024 the authors report a 1.24 Sharpe at constant size and 1.33 with volatility targeting, after $0.0035/share commission and slippage, with a 37–43% hit rate. Results improve at higher VIX and with a volatility multiplier near 1.3–1.5 (they keep 1.0). These are the authors' figures, not independently verified, and they are for shares, not options. Implemented as `noise-v1`; see the README for deviations.
- **First-half-hour predicts last-half-hour momentum** ([Gao, Han, Li & Zhou, JFE 2018](https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/attention-prop-traders-the-first-half-hour-of-trading-predicts-the-last-half-hour/)) holds for SPY and several international index futures, with mixed later evidence. No NIFTY-specific test was found. A 15–30-minute hold at the session end leaves little room for option spread, theta and per-order charges. Not implemented.
- **ORB on NIFTY**: available material is practitioner code and videos (for example [Marketcalls](https://www.marketcalls.in/amibroker/backtestable-open-range-breakout-orb-study-for-amibroker.html)), not a cost-inclusive study. Audits of US-options ORB backtests report that realistic execution removes much of the apparent edge.
- **Not implemented:** option selling captures the better-documented volatility risk premium, but it needs far more margin than ₹25,000 and a multi-leg engine. VWAP-based rules need futures volume because the index has none.

Every candidate needs NIFTY evidence: paper forward results and replay over recorded bid/ask, including the 0/10/25 bps scenarios. Run both strategies on the same sessions with `python3 -m app.backtest <db> --strategy noise` once at least 14 sessions of candles are saved.
